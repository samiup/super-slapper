Privacy Policy

Super Slapper Max Pro Ultra · Effective 22 November 2026

The short version: the game collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it sends nothing about you to us or to anyone else. Everything it remembers, including any face photo you add, is kept on your own device and stays there.

Who we are

Super Slapper Max Pro Ultra is published by West Town Products LLC ("we"). Questions about this policy: support@westtownholding.com.

What the game keeps on your device

  • Your progress: levels unlocked, stars, best scores, badges, career statistics, and which tips you have already seen.
  • Your settings: sound, music and vibration switches, and the glove and flame colours you picked.
  • Faces you add (only if you use "Add a face"): see the next section.

This is saved in the app's own storage on your phone or tablet, or in your browser's storage for this site if you play on the web. We have no copy of it and no way to read it.

Face photos ("Add a face")

The game lets you put a face on an enemy. It is optional, and the game works the same without it.

  • What happens to the photo. You pick a picture or take one with your device's own camera app. The game finds the face in it, then keeps only a small cropped picture of that face. The original photo is not kept by the game.
  • Where it is processed. Entirely on your device. The face-finding software is built into the game and works without a connection. The photo, the cropped face and anything worked out from them are never uploaded, never sent to us and never shared with anyone.
  • What is worked out from it. Where the eyes, nose, mouth and outline of the face are, so the picture can be fitted to a cartoon head and made to pull expressions; and a rough guess at whether to use a male or female cartoon body, which you can change. These are used for nothing else. The game does not recognise or identify anyone, and does not compare faces with each other or with any database.
  • What is saved. The cropped face picture, the positions of its features, the name you type for it (if any), the body choice, and whether it is switched on.
  • How long. Until you delete it. Open "Add a face", choose the face, and tap TAKE THEM OUT; it is erased at once. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game (or clearing this site's data in a browser) erases every face and all progress.
  • Camera. The game never uses the camera itself and does not ask for camera permission. "Take a photo" hands over to your device's camera app, and the game receives only the one picture you take.
  • Backups. The Android app is set so that its data, including faces, is left out of your device's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Other people's faces. Please only add a photo of someone who has agreed to it. You are responsible for the photos you choose to add.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or other sensors beyond touch and vibration.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information: we do not have any.

If you play in a web browser, the company hosting the website may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address and the time of a visit) as every website host does. The game itself adds nothing to them.

Playing on a game portal

The game is also offered on game portals such as CrazyGames. A portal is run by another company under its own privacy policy: it may measure how its games are played, and if you are signed in to an account there it may store your game progress (levels, scores and trophies) with that account so it follows you between devices. The version of the game on those portals does not have the face photo feature, so no photo is involved.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. A child should ask a parent or guardian before adding anyone's photo.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated here and its date changed before that version of the game is released.
